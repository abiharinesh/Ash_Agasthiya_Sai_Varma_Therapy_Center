Practitioner Background

Traditional Knowledge. Dedicated Practice.

Rajesh Ramalingam is the resident practitioner at Ash Agasthiya Sai Varma Therapy Center in Vilathikulam, identified in the center's materials as a spiritual healer. Dedicated to preserving and practicing classical Tamil wellness sciences, he has undergone comprehensive training in Varma Kalai (vital point manipulation) and Thokkanam (traditional therapeutic massage).

His approach prioritises personalized, holistic attention for each individual who walks through our doors. Emphasising physical relaxation, energetic harmony, and mental calmness, sessions are conducted with deep respect for traditional lineages, individual privacy, and clear boundaries.

Sacred Lineage & Spiritual Mentorship

With Sri G.N. Mahalingam Ayya

Spiritual Healer Rajesh Ramalingam has received profound traditional guidance as the direct disciple (சிஷ்யர்) of revered master Sri G.N. Mahalingam Ayya. Guided by traditional spiritual initiation, he focuses purely on spiritual healing, subtle biofield alignment, and connecting families with their ancestral deities.
