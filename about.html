Our Tradition, Our Approach

A traditional wellness center rooted in Tamil heritage

Ash Agasthiya Sai Varma Therapy Center (ASH) is a traditional wellness center in Vilathikulam, Thoothukudi District, Tamil Nadu. We focus on Varma Kalai, Thokkanam, traditional massage, meditation, yoga, and spiritual wellness practices. Our approach honours time-tested Tamil traditions while providing personalised care in a welcoming environment.

  • Respect for traditional knowledge and practices
  • Personalised attention for every visitor
  • A welcoming and calm environment
  • Clear and honest communication about services
  • Responsible, transparent service descriptions
