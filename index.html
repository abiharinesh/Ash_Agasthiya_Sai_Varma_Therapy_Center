Our Tradition, Our Approach

Ash Agasthiya Sai Varma Therapy Center (ASH) is a traditional wellness center focused on Varma Kalai, Thokkanam, massage, meditation, yoga, and spiritual wellness practices. We honour time-tested Tamil traditions while providing personalised care in a welcoming environment.
