Varma Kalai is an ancient Tamil martial and healing science that focuses on vital energy points located across the human body. In traditional wellness practice, trained practitioners apply careful pressure or stimulation to specific points to release physical tension, restore balanced vitality, and support holistic relaxation. At Ash Agasthiya Sai, it is practiced as a complementary traditional art.
