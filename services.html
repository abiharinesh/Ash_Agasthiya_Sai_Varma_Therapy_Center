Traditional Wellness Services

Explore our complete range of traditional services

Traditional Varma Kalai

Varma Kalai is an ancient Tamil traditional practice that focuses on vital energy points (varma points) in the body. Our practitioner applies specialised techniques aimed at supporting general wellness and relaxation. This is a traditional practice and is not intended as a substitute for medical treatment.

Thokkanam & Traditional Massage

Traditional Tamil massage techniques intended for relaxation and general wellness. Thokkanam involves specific hand movements and pressure techniques. Suitability depends on individual circumstances – please consult with us before your session.

Women's Wellness Massage

Dedicated wellness massage services for women, provided with full privacy, consent, and professional boundaries. Our sessions are designed to promote relaxation and general well-being in a safe, respectful environment.

Postnatal & Newborn Oil Massage

Traditional oil massage for new mothers and newborns, offered with appropriate care and precautions. Newborn massage is offered when appropriate and with guidance from a qualified paediatric healthcare professional. These services do not replace medical care.

Relaxation & Head Massage

A soothing head and body relaxation massage designed to ease everyday tension and promote a sense of calm. This is a relaxation service and is not intended to treat migraines, chronic headaches, or neurological conditions.
